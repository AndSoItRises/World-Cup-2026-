Key Findings
ELO is the dominant signal

elo_diff ranks #1 in XGBoost feature importance by a wide margin. Strength differential predicts outcomes — but not precisely. Every other feature is marginal by comparison.

Squad quality was the proven missing signal — and we found it

V4 diagnostic confirmed squad market value carries +0.178 incremental R² beyond existing features. We sourced it via FIFA/EA video-game ratings (2005→FC26), leakage-safe via backward asof merge. squad_strength_diff landed at feature rank #3.

CONCACAF inflation — real and partially fixed

V3: Mexico overrated by +10pp vs market. Root cause: ELO inflates from wins against weak opponents. V4 reduced this to +6.1pp and lifted model↔market correlation from 0.665 → 0.838.

Draw recall is structurally capped at ~9%

Across all versions, draw recall cannot exceed ~9% without simultaneously overforecasting non-draws. This appears to be a ceiling given the available features — draws are fundamentally hard to predict.

The model is now largely aligned with markets

V4's 0.838 model↔market correlation means most pricing signal is captured. Remaining divergences (Mexico +6pp, France −7.7pp) are concentrated known biases, not actionable edges — no historical odds backtest has validated them.
